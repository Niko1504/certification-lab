# Font licences

Geologica, Golos Text, Martian Mono, Prata, Onest, PT Mono, Wix Madefor Display,
Nunito Sans and JetBrains Mono are all licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The files here are WOFF2 subsets (Cyrillic and Latin only) fetched from the Google
Fonts API by `scripts/vendor-fonts.mjs` and redistributed unmodified.

Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

Reserved font names are unchanged; nothing here is sold on its own. A site built with
this skill copies the files of its chosen pair into its own `fonts/` folder and links
them from there. Nothing is loaded from Google Fonts at runtime.
